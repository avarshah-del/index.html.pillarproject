Cup catch

Sit across from each other. Roll a ball. Cover it with a cup. Roll it back.

What you need

  • A ping-pong ball, or any small ball that rolls.
  • Two cups for you and two cups for the other person.
  • Cups in two colors, if you have them.
  • A table you can both reach.

Steps

  1. Sit so you can see each other's face.
  2. Roll the ball slowly across the table.
  3. Cover the ball with one cup. Tip the cup, and roll the ball back.
  4. Take turns. Red cup, blue cup, or whichever color you feel like calling.

A kindness tip

Keep the ball on the table. If it hops off, pick it up and set it back down. Nobody has to chase it.
