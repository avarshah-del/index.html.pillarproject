Become a Memory Care Ally

Step up, earn service hours, and make a massive difference in your community. We are mobilizing Gen Z to bridge the generational gap in dementia care.

Apply to Volunteer

What Does an Ally Do?

🎨

Lead Engagement

You will run art, music, and tactile sensory sessions for memory care residents at our partner facilities, bringing them joy and cognitive stimulation.

🛡️

Provide Respite

By engaging the patients, you are directly giving their family caregivers a dedicated 1-2 hour window to step away, breathe, and avoid burnout.

🎓

Learn & Grow

No prior experience needed! We provide you with the training and empathetic communication skills necessary to interact safely and compassionately.
