Mandatory Training: The Empowered Caregiver

Safety and empathy are our top priorities. Before interacting with residents, all applicants must complete the Alzheimer’s Association's official training course to understand person-centered care.

Access Training Portal ↗

* You must upload your Certificate of Completion to earn your official Memory Care Ally status.

What Does an Ally Do?

Lead Engagement

You will run art, music, and tactile sensory sessions for memory care residents at our partner facilities, bringing them joy and cognitive stimulation.

Provide Respite

By engaging the patients, you are directly giving their family caregivers a dedicated 1-2 hour window to step away, breathe, and avoid burnout.

Learn & Grow

Beyond the online training, we provide you with on-site shadowing and the empathetic communication skills necessary to interact confidently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior experience with dementia?

Not at all. We require all volunteers to complete The Empowered Caregiver course online, and we provide on-site shadowing before you ever lead a session on your own.

How do I verify my service hours?

Once you are an official Memory Care Ally, you will receive access to our volunteer portal where a site supervisor will sign off on your logged hours for NHS, college apps, or graduation requirements.

How old do I have to be?

We currently accept high school and college students (ages 14-22). If you are under 18, you will need a parent or guardian to sign a standard liability waiver before your first visit.
