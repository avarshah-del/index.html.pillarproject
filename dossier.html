CASE ALZHEIMER'S

MISSION BRIEFING & PARTNERSHIP OVERVIEW // AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY

1. THE COMMANDER'S BRIEFING

Agent, we have a critical mission. Millions of children are facing a family Alzheimer's diagnosis without the intel they need to understand it. They see their loved ones changing, but no one is explaining the biology behind it in a way they can grasp. We are changing that today.

2. THE GADGET (About the Book)

Case Alzheimer's is an "informational fiction" spy-thriller for kids ages 5-10. Here is how the mission unfolds:

PHASE 1: The Diagnosis

Children notice behavioral changes in their loved ones. Confusion and fear set in. Agent H.M. is called to investigate.

PHASE 2: Infiltrating Brain HQ

Agent H.M. shrinks down and enters the brain, discovering regions like the Amygdala ("Emotion-Land") and Broca's Area ("The Word Warehouse").

PHASE 3: Identifying the Threat

The science is revealed: Amyloid plaques block the neural tracks, and Tau Tangles act as weeds, causing synaptic failure and glitches.

PHASE 4: Mission Accomplished

With the mystery solved, the fear is gone. Children learn exactly how to support their loved ones with empathy and patience.

  • Verified Intel: Supported by dementia-education leaders Teepa Snow and Lori La Bey, Golden Globe Member Anke Hoffman, renowned children's author Alice-Kate Lamb, and leaders at HFC (Hilarity for Charity).
