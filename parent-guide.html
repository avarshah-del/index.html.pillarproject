Parent & Educator Guide

Explaining memory loss to children doesn't have to be scary. This guide translates the complex science of Alzheimer's into an empowering spy adventure.

How to Use the Book

1

Adopt the Persona

Refer to the child as "Agent." Treat the book like a top-secret briefing file. Framing the disease as a "glitch" in a high-tech system removes fear and replaces it with curiosity.

2

Pause for Empathy

As you read through the different brain zones, ask the child: "How would you help your grandparent if this system glitched?" Remind them that patience is their best gadget.

3

Play the Games

After reading, use the secret code at the back of the book to unlock the Digital Training Zone and the HQ Rec Room for real-world activities.

The Science Translated

A quick cheat-sheet so you can confidently answer your child's questions about the brain's anatomy.

The Data Library

Hippocampus

The Metaphor: The brain's giant vault of memories stored like books on shelves.

When a kid asks: "Why do they remember when they were little, but not yesterday?"

Your answer: "In Alzheimer's, the glitch hits the front of the library first, where the newest books are kept. The old books in the back are safer for longer."

The Word Warehouse

Broca & Wernicke

The Metaphor: The factory where language and sentences are built.

When a kid asks: "Why did grandpa call a pen a spoon?"

Your answer: "The glitch (Aphasia) tangled the delivery system in the Word Warehouse. He knows what he wants to say, but the wrong word fell off the shelf."

Emotion Land

Amygdala

The Metaphor: The emotional roller coaster and alarm center.

When a kid asks: "Why is grandma scared when she forgets something?"

Your answer: "Even when the Data Library glitches and she forgets the facts, her Emotion Land is still working perfectly. She can still feel love and fear, so she needs a hug!"

Brain HQ

Prefrontal Cortex

The Metaphor: The boss's office where big decisions are made.

When a kid asks: "Why did they put their keys in the fridge?"

Your answer: "The boss in Brain HQ is having a hard time sorting out logic because of the tangles. It's not their fault; the system is just a bit confused."
