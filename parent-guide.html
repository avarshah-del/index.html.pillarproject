A Reading & Teaching Resource for Families

Talking to kids about Alzheimer’s starts with meeting them at their age.

A four-year-old notices tone of voice and hugs; a ten-year-old wants to know why the brain's filing system is glitching; a teenager often carries quiet questions about how to help. The book is written for ages 6–12. The guides below stay with a child who is younger or older than that, and with the 6–12 readers the book was written for. Select a bracket to open a tailored reading guide for Case Alzheimer's.

1. Choose Your Child’s Age Group

Select a bracket below to open the step-by-step reading & conversation guide.

Under 5 AGES <5 →

Picture-walk approach focusing on feelings, gentle touch, and reassurance that Grandma or Grandpa's heart still loves them.

Ages 5–8 EARLY READERS →

Using the "Brain HQ" and "Emotion-Land" characters as a read-aloud story to remove fear when a loved one repeats questions.

Ages 9–12 CORE BOOK AGENTS →

Deep-diving into how neurons, the Data Library, and Tangles work—plus how to use the Digital Training Zone and Rec Room games.

Ages 13+ TEENS & ALLIES →

Translating the book's metaphors into real neuroscience, co-regulation techniques, and supporting primary caregivers at home.

The Brain HQ Database: Agent H.M.’s Glossary

Each entry pairs the Book Term with its real Biological Term. Hover over (or tap) any row to reveal the definition from the book.

Brain HQ Prefrontal Cortex
The boss of the brain that makes big decisions and solves puzzles.
The Data Library Hippocampus
The brain's memory saver that grabs new memories and files them away.
Emotion-Land Amygdala
The part of the brain that controls your feelings and emotions.
The Word Warehouse Broca's & Wernicke's Areas
The language factories that help us build words and talk to each other.
A Class-8 Word Glitch! Aphasia
A condition that makes it hard to find the right words or speak.
The Big Jump Synaptic Gap
The tiny empty space between brain cells that messages must jump across.
Chemical Messengers Neurotransmitters
Chemicals that carry messages across the Synaptic Gap.
High-Speed Zap Action Potential
A high-speed zap of electricity that shoots messages down a brain cell.
The Launch Pad & Catchers Axon Terminal & Dendrites
The edge where messages jump off (Axon) and the landing pads that catch them (Dendrites).
Speed Boosters Myelin
A special wrapping that helps brain messages zoom super fast.
Cellular Energy ATP Fuel
The energy that powers every cell in your body.
Clean-Up Crew Proteasome Unit
The brain's clean-up crew that clears out the garbage.
The Weeds Tangles
Twisted proteins in Alzheimer's that block brain tracks like weeds.
The Deep Archives Long-Term Memory
Safe vaults where your oldest, favorite memories are stored forever.
