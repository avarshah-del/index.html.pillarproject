CASE ALZHEIMER'S

MISSION BRIEFING & PARTNERSHIP OVERVIEW // AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY

1. THE COMMANDER'S BRIEFING

Children who watch a loved one change often never get an explanation they can follow. Case Alzheimer's puts the biology into a spy story they can hold onto.

2. THE GADGET (About the Book)

Case Alzheimer's is an informational-fiction spy story for ages 6–12. Here is how the mission unfolds:

PHASE 1: The Mission at Brain HQ

Agent H.M. is called to the Prefrontal Cortex by Big Boss. The brain is glitching, files have disappeared, and mysterious tangles are taking over!

PHASE 2: The Word Warehouse

At Broca's and Wernicke's areas, H.M. discovers a "Class-8 Aphasia" where tangles are strangling the glowing word-orbs.

PHASE 3: Emotion-Land

Jumping the Synaptic Gap on a Signal Scooter, H.M. arrives at the Amygdala to find the rollercoaster rides out of whack and weeds causing emotional glitches.

PHASE 4: Saving the Archives

At the Hippocampus Data Library, the New Data Section is completely blocked by weeds. While new memories can't be saved, H.M. discovers the Deep Archives of old memories are still safe!

  • Who stands with the book: Formally endorsed by Teepa Snow and Illana Raia. The project has collaborated with Lori La Bey, Alice-Kate Lamb, Anke Hoffman, and HFC (Hilarity for Charity).

3. WHY CASE ALZHEIMER'S? (PARTNER BENEFITS)

Why should your organization fund this mission?

  • A book for children: Many dementia resources are written for adults. This one is for ages 6–12 and the families reading with them.
  • Support a student author: The book is by Ava Shah, a Livingston student and TED-Ed speaker, written for children who are watching someone they love change.
  • What the money does: It pays for printed books. Educator guides are already on this site. Funded copies go to classrooms and families.

4. ACTIVE FIELD OPS (The School Pilot)
