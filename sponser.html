CASE ALZHEIMER'S

MISSION BRIEFING & PARTNERSHIP OVERVIEW // AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY

1. THE COMMANDER's BRIEFING

Agent, we have a critical mission. Millions of children are facing a family Alzheimer's diagnosis without the intel they need to understand it. They see their loved ones changing, but no one is explaining the biology behind it in a way they can grasp. We are changing that today.

2. THE GADGET (About the Book)

Case Alzheimer's is an "informational fiction" spy-thriller for kids ages 5-10. We translate complex neurobiology into highly accessible concepts:

  • The Science: Amyloid plaques block the tracks, and Tau Tangles (weeds) invade the cells causing synaptic failure.
  • The Empathy: Demystifies behavioral changes by exploring the Amygdala ("Emotion-Land") and Broca's Area ("The Word Warehouse").
  • Verified Intel: Formally endorsed by global dementia leaders Teepa Snow and Lori La Bey.

3. ACTIVE FIELD OPS (The School Pilot)

We are launching our curriculum this November across local elementary schools in Livingston, NJ. We are bringing the book and an interactive STEM/Neuroscience Author Visit to 4th, 5th, and 6th-grade science classrooms. Teachers receive a zero-prep Educator Guide to merge human anatomy standards with empathy building.
