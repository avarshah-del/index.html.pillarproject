Feelings wheel

Some feelings are easier to point at than to say. Make a wheel, then point.

Placeholder video - our own coming soon.

What you need

  • A paper plate, or a circle drawn on paper.
  • Four colors, crayons, or markers.
  • A quiet spot at the table.

Steps

  1. Draw two lines so the circle has four parts.
  2. Label them in your own words: sun, cloud, rain, and fog. Add a face if you like.
  3. Color each part.
  4. Point to the part that matches how the room feels. Stay with that one for a minute. You can point again later.

A kindness tip

Pointing counts as the sentence. You do not have to explain. If the feeling changes, the wheel can change too.
