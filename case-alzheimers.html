HQ REC ROOM: GAMES & MISSIONS

Agents! Use these cognitively stimulating, fun activities to connect with grandparents or loved ones experiencing memory loss. They are perfectly designed to spark joy and bypass brain glitches.

🎈

BALLOON VOLLEYBALL

A safe, seated physical activity that improves hand-eye coordination. It encourages gentle movement, crossing the midline of the body, and almost always results in a lot of laughter!

🎵

MUSICAL TIME MACHINE

Play popular songs from when your loved one was in their teens and 20s. Music pathways often remain perfectly intact in the brain, sparking deep memories and spontaneous singalongs!

🧺

SORTING MISSIONS

Sort a basket of colorful socks, organize a toolbox by shape, or fold hand towels. These tasks provide tactile stimulation and give a wonderful, calming sense of purpose and accomplishment.

🤝

FOR ADULT ALLIES & CAREGIVERS

HOW TO USE THIS BOOK

Read Case: Alzheimer's together with your child. Use the spy theme as a safe, analytical framework to explain why Grandma or Grandpa might act differently. By turning medical symptoms into "glitches," we remove the fear of the unknown and replace it with curiosity and empathy.

ANSWERING TOUGH QUESTIONS

Kids will naturally ask if they can "catch" Alzheimer's or if you will get it. Assure them it is not contagious like a cold. Remind them that right now, their only mission is to be the best Secret Agent of joy they can be. Focus their energy on what they can control: their patience and kindness.

SEEKING RESPITE

You cannot pour from an empty cup. If you are experiencing caregiver burnout, it is critical to find moments to step away. Explore our main Pillar Project hub to learn more about localized volunteer respite and support group networks.
