Cookie mission

You find the ingredients. You stir. A grown-up handles the oven. Then you share.

Placeholder video - our own coming soon.

What you need

  • The two recipe cards on this page.
  • A bowl and a spoon.
  • The ingredients on the card.
  • A grown-up for the oven.

Steps

  1. Read one ingredient out loud. Find it together.
  2. Add it to the bowl. Stir until it looks like the card.
  3. The grown-up heats the oven, puts the pan in, and takes it out.
  4. Wait until the cookies are cool. Then share one at the table.

A kindness tip

The oven and the hot pan belong to the grown-up. Hands stay on the spoon and the bowl until the cookies are cool enough to touch.
