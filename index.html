Bridging the Gap in Memory Care

When Alzheimer's impacts a family, primary caregivers shoulder immense burnout. The Pillar Project is a Gen Z-led initiative providing grassroots, intergenerational respite through our "Divide and Pamper" model.

Case Alzheimers: A Brain HQ Mystery

Publishing officially in November for National Alzheimer's Awareness Month!

Explaining memory loss to children can be terrifying for young families. Instead of a scary medical talk, this book takes kids on an action-packed spy adventure inside "Brain HQ" alongside Agent H.M. Through fun metaphors, children learn how the brain works, why memories get misplaced, and how to show empathy to family members facing dementia.

Our "Divide & Pamper" Model

🧠

Sensory Engagement

Trained high school "Memory Care Allies" lead interactive music, art, and tactile sessions for memory care patients, bringing joy and dignity back to aging.

☕

Caregiver Respite

While patients are engaged, primary family caregivers receive dedicated, guilt-free time to step away, breathe, and relax.

🎁

Wellness Baskets

Caregivers receive wellness totes filled with stress-relief journals, herbal teas, and self-care tools to support their mental health.

Caregiver & Certification Resources

Navigating eldercare can be isolating, and providing care requires deep empathy. Here are trusted organizations offering support, education, and official training.

Support & Community

Alzheimer's Association ↗

24/7 helpline, local support groups, and comprehensive educational resources regarding dementia.

Caregiver Action Network ↗

The nation's leading family caregiver organization working to improve the quality of life for those who care for loved ones.

Daughterhood.org ↗

A community and podcast designed to validate and support women navigating the eldercare system for their parents.

Family Caregiver Alliance (FCA) ↗

Information, support, and specialized resources specifically geared toward families caring for adults with cognitive disorders.

Training & Certification

essentiALZ® Certification ↗

The Alzheimer's Association's official individual certification recognizing knowledge of quality dementia care practices.

NCCDP Certified Dementia Practitioner ↗

The National Council of Certified Dementia Practitioners offers the global standard in CDP certification for care professionals.

Positive Approach to Care® (PAC) ↗

Teepa Snow's widely recognized training modules focused on practical, compassionate strategies for engaging with memory care patients.

UCLA Dementia Care Video Series ↗

A free, high-quality educational series perfect for young volunteers looking to understand the behavioral realities of dementia.
