Our "Divide & Pamper" Model

Co-Regulation

Through gentle sensory engagement and shared calm, our trained high school allies help memory care patients achieve emotional balance, fostering a grounded, peaceful environment.

Caregiver Respite

While patients are safely and actively engaged, primary family caregivers receive dedicated, guilt-free time to step away, breathe, and recharge their own batteries.

Intergenerational Connection

Bridging the gap between Gen Z and seniors, we foster meaningful, one-on-one relationships that bring joy and dignity back to aging while instilling deep empathy in our youth volunteers.

Brain HQ Glossary

Learn the Spy Terms

Brain HQ

Scientific name: Prefrontal Cortex

The boss of the brain. It sits right behind your forehead and makes all the big decisions and solves puzzles.

Data Library

Scientific name: Hippocampus

The brain's giant memory vault. It stores all your favorite memories like books on a shelf.

Emotion Land

Scientific name: Amygdala

The alarm center. It controls your feelings and tells you when to be scared, happy, or excited.

Microglia

Scientific name: Glial Cells

The brain's immune system security agents. They sweep up glitches and keep the neurons healthy.
