Case Alzheimers:
A Brain HQ Mystery

Explaining memory loss to children can be terrifying for young families. Instead of a scary medical talk, this book takes kids on an action-packed spy adventure inside "Brain HQ" to learn how memories work and how to show empathy to loved ones facing dementia.

Demystifying Dementia for Kids

When my grandfather was diagnosed with Alzheimer's, I struggled to understand the changes happening to him. Children often feel scared or confused when a grandparent forgets their name or acts differently. Case Alzheimers was written to bridge that gap. By following Agent H.M. through the filing cabinets of the mind, kids learn that while the brain might misplace a file, the love behind it is never lost.
