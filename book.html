Demystifying Dementia for Kids

When my grandfather was diagnosed with Alzheimer's, I struggled to understand the changes happening to him. Children often feel scared or confused when a grandparent forgets their name or acts differently. Case Alzheimer's was written to bridge that gap. By following Agent H.M. through the filing cabinets of the mind, kids learn that while the brain might misplace a file, the love behind it is never lost.
