Color radio

A color can say the whole thing. Hold a card up. Point back.

Placeholder video - our own coming soon.

What you need

  • Three pieces of paper or cards: red, yellow, and blue.
  • A table between you.
  • The cups from cup catch, if you want to match a color to a cup.

Steps

  1. Lay the cards where you can both see them.
  2. Pick one card and hold it up. You can say the color, or just show it.
  3. The other person points to the card, or covers a cup of that color.
  4. Trade jobs. The person who was pointing gets to hold the next card.

A kindness tip

If the word will not come, that is all right. The color is enough. Smile, and try the next card.
