FOR EDUCATORS

One learning goal for each zone, in the words the zone already teaches. Students with the book still use the printed code. This preview does not replace it.

  1. Brain HQ, prefrontal cortex. Point behind the forehead. This part makes the big decisions and solves puzzles. Alzheimer's is the condition causing the tangles and glitches.
  2. Word Warehouse, Broca's and Wernicke's areas. These areas build words and talk. Aphasia is the glitch that makes the right word hard to find. If a word drops, be kind and patient.
  3. Emotion Land, amygdala. This part controls feelings. When someone is scared or confused, gentle words and a comforting hug help. Feelings stay real even when memories slip.
  4. Data Library, hippocampus. This memory vault is shaped like a tiny seahorse. New things are usually forgotten first. Photo albums and old songs are how you help someone remember happily.
