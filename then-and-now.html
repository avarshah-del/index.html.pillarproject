Then and now

Two pictures. One from a while ago, and one from today. Look, and tell one small thing.

Placeholder video - our own coming soon.

What you need

  • One older photo. A printed picture is easier to hold than a phone.
  • One photo from today, or this year.
  • A table, and enough time that nobody feels rushed.

Steps

  1. Put the older photo down first.
  2. Each person says one thing they notice. A color, a hat, a smile, a place.
  3. Then look at today's photo the same way.
  4. You can stop after one photo. One good look is a whole visit.

A kindness tip

This is not a test. If a name is missing, stay with the part they do remember. If a picture feels sad, set it aside and pick another.
